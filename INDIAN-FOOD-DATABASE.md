## How to Use

### Finding Foods
Search by common name:
- Type "dal" → See all dal types
- Type "roti" → See all bread types
- Type "paneer" → See paneer dishes

### Entering Quantities
- **Breads:** Enter number (2 = 2 rotis)
- **Vegetables:** Enter grams (200 = 200g bhindi)
- **Milk/Curd:** Enter grams (200 = 200g curd)
- **Lentils/Dal:** Enter grams (150 = 150g cooked dal)

### Examples

**Lunch with dal and rice:**
1. Tap "🍽️ Lunch"
2. Search "dal" → Select "dal (red lentils cooked)"
3. Enter: 150 (meaning 150g of cooked dal)
4. Click "Add"
5. Search "basmati rice"
6. Enter: 150 (meaning 150g of cooked rice)
7. Click "Add"

**Breakfast with roti and sabzi:**
1. Tap "☀️ Breakfast"
2. Search "roti"
3. Enter: 2 (meaning 2 rotis)
4. Click "Add"

## Nutrition Tips for Indian Diet

### High Protein Combinations
- **Rajma + Rice:** 200g + 150g = 380 cal, 39.7g protein ✓
- **Dal + Roti:** 150g + 2 rotis = 316 cal, 19.4g protein ✓
- **Paneer + Roti:** 100g + 2 rotis = 355 cal, 30.6g protein ✓

### Low Calorie Vegetables
- Lauki, Karela, Brinjal, Cabbage (all <35 cal per 100g)
- Great for volume eating without excess calories

### Complete Proteins
Combine:
- **Dal + Rice** = Complete amino acid profile
- **Paneer + Roti** = High quality protein
- **Rajma + Rice** = Natural pairing with good protein

### Cooking Oil Considerations
- 1 tbsp Ghee = 125 cal (add to your total)
- 1 tbsp Mustard oil = 125 cal
- 1 tbsp Coconut oil = 125 cal
- Track the oil used in cooking!
